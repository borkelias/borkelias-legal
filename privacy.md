# Borkelias — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how Borkelias, a Discord music bot, handles information when you use its commands and features.

## 2. Information We May Process
Depending on how the bot is implemented, Borkelias may process:
- Discord server and channel IDs to provide music playback.
- User IDs to identify users who issue commands.
- Music search queries and playback queue information to operate music features.
- Technical logs and error information to maintain and troubleshoot the service.

## 3. How Information Is Used
Information is used to provide music playback, respond to commands, maintain queues, troubleshoot errors, and protect the bot from abuse.

## 4. Data Storage and Sharing
We do not intentionally sell users' personal information. Data may be processed by Discord and any third-party services required for music playback. Information is retained only as necessary for the bot's operation and legitimate maintenance needs.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion
If you have questions about information associated with your use of Borkelias or wish to request deletion, contact the developer through the bot's support server or official contact method.

## 6. Security
Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by the bot. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The latest version will be published at the same URL.

## 8. Contact
For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact the bot's developer through the support server or the contact information provided on the bot's official page.
